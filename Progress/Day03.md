## 💡 Approaches

### 4. Median of Two Sorted Arrays

- Solved the problem by merging the two sorted arrays.
- Found the median from the merged sorted array.
- Focused on correctness and understanding the problem before optimizing.
- This approach is straightforward and easy to implement.

---

### 1464. Maximum Product of Two Elements in an Array

- Traversed the array to identify the two largest elements.
- Computed the answer using the formula:

```java
(max1 - 1) * (max2 - 1)
```

- Achieved the result in a single traversal without sorting the array.
